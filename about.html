About Tea&Tusk

Our story

Built around relationships

Tea&Tusk is a UK-based tea and lifestyle company focused on premium teas from Sri Lanka, Japan and China, alongside carefully selected Japanese craft and ornaments.

We are building the business around direct, respectful supplier relationships, honest origin stories and transparent support for ethical elephant welfare.

The company combines commercial discipline and responsible sourcing with modern social storytelling led by the next generation.
