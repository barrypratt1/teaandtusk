Our purpose

Better futures for elephants

Our aim is to build partnerships that support ethical care, education and long-term welfare.

Partnership in development

Support that can be seen

Tea&Tusk is seeking a formal partnership with a credible elephant welfare organisation in Sri Lanka. We visited the Elephant Freedom Project and were moved by its work; any formal relationship will be announced only after both parties agree the terms.

  • Clear written partnership terms
  • Transparent donation mechanism
  • Permission-based use of names and imagery
  • Regular impact updates

Credibility before claims

Until a partnership is signed, we will not claim endorsement, use partner logos, or state a donation amount. Our intention is to build something durable and honest.
